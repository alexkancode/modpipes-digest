User bible: itinerary

A shared trip and event itinerary app. Owners create plans with timed, located activities, photos, Pinterest pins, to-dos and reminders, and invite others by email or phone.

Audience
Organisers of group trips or events, and the invitees who follow the plan.
Identity
Supabase Auth: email magic link or phone SMS code; cookie session via @supabase/ssr; roles owner and participant (itinerary_participants.role); owner-only invites. No site-wide admin role.

Proto-personas

Each persona is a hypothesis drawn from the code, not from user research.

Trip organiser

Code-grounded proto-persona: a hypothesis from the code, not research.

Signed-in owner of an itinerary (itinerary_participants.role = owner). Creates plans, fills in activities and invites others; the only role that sees the invite panel.

When I am planning a group trip or event, I want to build a plan with timed, located activities and invite everyone by email or phone, so I can keep the whole group on the same schedule.

Paths

  • Create a plan, add activities and invite the group (happy)
  • First visit with no plans yet (detour)
  • Save a new plan that fails (detour)
  • Add an activity that fails validation (detour)
  • Invite someone and get an error (detour)
  • Attach a photo or Pinterest pin to an activity (detour)
  • Leave Preview for the plan list (dead-end)
Evidence and reasoning for persona-trip-organiser

Owner-only invite check and invite panel make this role explicit in code.

Plan maintainer

Code-grounded proto-persona: a hypothesis from the code, not research.

The owner returning after creation to change the plan: edits or deletes activities, removes people from the invite list and removes photos or pins. Only the owner may delete participants (RLS). No separate manager role exists, so this is the owner role at a later moment.

When the plan changes or someone should no longer see it, I want to edit activities and remove invitees, so I can keep the plan accurate for the group.

Paths

  • Return to a plan, change activities and the guest list, then check it (happy)
  • Delete an activity or remove a participant (detour)
  • Delete a whole plan that is no longer needed (dead-end)
Evidence and reasoning for persona-plan-maintainer

Participant deletion and the owner-only delete policy are in code, but the code has no role beyond owner and participant, so this shares the organiser's role and differs in situation.

Invited participant

Code-grounded proto-persona: a hypothesis from the code, not research.

Signed-in person with the participant role who follows a plan someone else owns. Reads the day-grouped feed and sets their own reminders.

When someone has invited me to a trip, I want to open the plan and see what is happening and when, so I can show up at the right place at the right time.

Paths

  • Open a plan someone else owns and read the schedule (happy)
  • Turn a reminder on or off for an activity (happy)
  • Open a plan with no activities yet (detour)
  • Sign in before any invite has been accepted or after being removed (detour)
  • Open a plan link they cannot see (detour)
  • Try to change the organiser's plan (dead-end)
  • Accept an emailed invite and open the plan (happy)
  • Sign in with the phone number the organiser invited and open the plan (happy)
Evidence and reasoning for persona-invited-participant

Home splits 'My Plans' from 'Invited To'; the plan page and feed are the read path for invitees.

Signed-out visitor

Code-grounded proto-persona: a hypothesis from the code, not research.

Anonymous visitor who reaches any URL. Middleware sends every page to the login screen, so this persona only ever sees sign-in. Two sign-in paths exist for the same person: an email magic link, or an SMS code for US, Mexico and Canada numbers.

When I follow an invite or open the site without a session, I want to sign in with my email or my phone number, so I can reach the plan I was invited to.

Paths

  • Sign in with an emailed magic link and reach the plan list (happy)
  • Follow a link to a specific plan while signed out (detour)
  • Use an expired or already-used magic link (detour)
  • Correct a mistyped email after sending (dead-end)
  • Sign in with an SMS code (happy)
  • Sign in with a number from another country (detour)
  • Enter a wrong or expired SMS code (detour)
Evidence and reasoning for persona-signed-out-visitor

No public page exists; the login page is the only thing an anonymous visitor can use. The phone user from round 1 is the same person at the same step, so it is folded in as a second sign-in path.

Open questions

  1. Which port and redirect URL should the app use: 3000, 3737 or 4000?

    Blocks a first-day developer. README.md:18 says http://localhost:3000; package.json:6 runs next dev on 3737; supabase/config.toml:154 sets site_url to 4000 and line 156 allows 3000 and 4000 but not 3737. Magic links and invite emails (inviteUserByEmail without redirectTo, src/app/api/invite/route.ts:81-82) both go to site_url, so sign-in and invite acceptance land where nothing runs.

  2. Where does a new developer get SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY?

    Blocks a first-day developer. /api/invite needs it (src/lib/supabase-admin.ts:7) but .env.example lists only the two public keys (.env.example:1-2). Without it every invite fails with a 500 (path-organiser-invite-fails).

  3. Which document holds the feature spec?

    Blocks a first-day developer. code-notes and routes.json cite documentation/README.md:1-15 for the promises, but that file holds only '# Legacy docs' (documentation/README.md:1). The feature text lives in README.md:3 and ProjectDescription.md. No tests exist to settle any of it.

  4. Where should a signed-out visitor land after signing in from a deep link?

    /auth/callback honours ?next= (src/app/auth/callback/route.ts:8) but middleware drops the requested path (src/middleware.ts:48-50) and the login form never sets next (src/app/login/page.tsx:49). An invitee clicking a plan link always lands on /.

  5. Should invited participants see the Edit page at all?

    Edit is shown to everyone on the plan page (src/app/itineraries/[id]/page.tsx:97-102) and is the only place reminders and to-dos live (src/components/item-list.tsx:161-162), yet only the invite panel is owner-gated (edit/page.tsx:145). The code does not say what a participant is meant to do there.

  6. How does an invitee learn they were invited?

    Phone invites send no SMS (src/app/api/invite/route.ts:147-153) and email invites to existing accounts send nothing (route.ts:78-79). Only new email addresses get a Supabase invite mail (route.ts:81-82).

  7. Who will deliver reminders, and through which channel?

    ProjectDescription.md:16 asks for reminders 30 minutes before in the activity's timezone; reminder-toggle.tsx:16 stores that default, but no sender exists in the repo.

  8. Is /itineraries/:id/view meant to replace the front page or be deleted?

    It duplicates src/app/itineraries/[id]/page.tsx minus the dates line and nothing links to it (src/app/itineraries/[id]/view/page.tsx:60-105).

  9. What happens with an invalid timezone on an activity?

    Timezone is free text (src/components/item-form.tsx:116); the feed passes it to toLocaleDateString (src/components/activity-feed.tsx:11-16), which throws a RangeError for an unknown zone. The code does not say whether the page then breaks.

  10. Is the day-by-day grouping meant to show?

    groupByDay is defined (src/components/activity-feed.tsx:8-22) but the render maps the flat list (line 41), while the README promises a line split by days (README.md:3).

  11. What does a removed participant see?

    The owner can remove a participant (src/components/invite-list.tsx:22-25); the person then sees the organiser-worded empty state 'Create one to get started' (src/app/page.tsx:69-74).

  12. Should deleting an activity or removing a person ask for confirmation?

    Both act at once with no confirm or undo (src/components/item-form.tsx:85-89; src/components/invite-list.tsx:22-25), and the removal ignores errors.

  13. What should happen if the owner-row insert fails after a plan is created?

    The participants insert result is ignored (src/components/itinerary-form.tsx:62-66) before redirecting to Edit; RLS visibility depends on that row (supabase/migrations/00004_fix_itineraries_rls_recursion.sql:13).
