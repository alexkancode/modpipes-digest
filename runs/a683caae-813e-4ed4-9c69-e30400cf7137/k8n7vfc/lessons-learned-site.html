Verdict

v2: why the gather fix improved the pipeline

Game forge art was stopped by a server restart and launched three times. Its lastSeconds was 919 s, but the model worked 278 s in all. v1 compared groups on lastSeconds, so art showed a 622 s lead and perf, the real slowest stage at 296 s, showed slack.

v2 adds busySeconds: the last launch for a looped stage, and the summed work for any other stage. Lead, slack, group wall and critical path all read busySeconds.

v3: why the floor fix was needed, and why it does not finish the job

The percent floor is a share of the whole run's groupWallSeconds. A long run therefore raises the bar for every stage. On Game forge under v1, 15 percent of 4232 s was 634.8 s. That exceeded game's whole 632 s lead, so no split of game could pass. The plan names that split as the known answer.

What the tester missed

1. v2 moved the denominator of the v3 floor

The tester justified v3 with 15 percent of 4232 s. That 4232 s came from v1 gather. The v2 edit, saved at 18:03:41, had already cut it to 3604 s. The v3 edit followed at 18:06:56. The conclusion held, but the reasoning used a stale number. The fix was a one-call replay, which also exposed the shift.

2. The skeptic's suggestions never reach the build

The stress skeptic halved the judge's saving from 110 s to about 55 s. The skeptic traced the cut to a second fetch wave, c14, in the disputes half. It then proposed one sentence asking that half to fetch in a single parallel wave, and marked the sentence "not a blocker". The build reads splits.json and verdicts.md, not verdict.md, and SKEPTIC-PASS ends the loop. The saved split lacks the sentence, so the smaller saving is the one to expect.

3. Time and lead now use different figures

The judge prompt still sets Time to lastLaunchSeconds, while v2 moved lead and slack to busySeconds. The two differ only for a restarted stage that is not looped. For art: 235 s against 278 s. The effect is small and conservative, but a future reader of the arithmetic will see two units.

4. The skeptic's saving does not travel

The report showed both the judge's 110 s and the skeptic's 55 s. splits.json holds only 110 s, and the saved split's description inherits nothing else. This needs no fix today. A reader of the saved pipeline alone sees the larger number.
