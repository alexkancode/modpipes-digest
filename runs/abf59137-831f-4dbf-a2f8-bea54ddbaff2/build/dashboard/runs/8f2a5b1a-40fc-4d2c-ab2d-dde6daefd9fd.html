Concept
You are a giant monster in a side-view neon night city. Walk, stomp buildings, swipe with a long animated reach, and fire a ranged attack (a breath beam) as tanks and helicopters attack. Health drains constantly, and every collapsed building (+10) or destroyed unit (+8) heals you, so destruction is survival. The army spawns at 0.6/s and ramps up, with none in the first 3 s and helicopters from 12 s. Health drains at 4/s. The stomp has a 140 px radius with shake 12, and buildings take 3 hits. Swipe reach is about 220 px and the ranged attack has about a 1.5 s cooldown; these two are untuned. The score is survival time plus destruction. The monster animates visibly on walk, stomp, swipe and breath. Use hit-stop, shake, dust, shockwave rings, combo popups and synthesised thuds and roars. The full bible is at /home/alex/.modpipes/runs/7a1088d1-12ce-4103-9966-8f87a4346cec/stages/bible/outputs/bible.md; read it first.
Scope
a small arcade game with one core loop, playable to a score in two to five minutes: one refilling city strip, played until death. A title screen and a game-over screen with restart.
Controls
keyboard and mouse on desktop. A/D walk, Space stomps, left-click swipes toward the cursor, and right-click or F fires the ranged attack toward the cursor. Touch is a nice-to-have.

Style
