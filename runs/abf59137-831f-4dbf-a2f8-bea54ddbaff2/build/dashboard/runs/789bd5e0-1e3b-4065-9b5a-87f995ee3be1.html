Concept
A tense, twitchy neon survival dodger in a tiny arena. One hit ends the run. Flying close past hazards without touching them (a graze, inside a 22 px ring) raises a score multiplier with a brief slow-mo dip and a tone that rises with the multiplier; the multiplier decays at 0.5 per second when you play safe. Bullet sprays from the start, telegraphed walls with one gap join at 45 s, winding-up chargers that lunge across the arena join at 90 s, dodged by movement only with no dash. Surviving 3 minutes wins; the score ranks the run. Pillars: danger is the reward; every death is readable; a run is over before you want it to be. The full bible is at /home/alex/.modpipes/runs/6ff46294-7e16-4648-88fc-c85825780091/stages/bible/outputs/bible.md; read it first.
