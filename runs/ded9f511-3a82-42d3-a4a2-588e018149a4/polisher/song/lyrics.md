# You Look Well

## [Verse 1]
Rain on the shelter roof like pennies,
you read the timetable, I read you.
Twenty years, your collar's up,
the left side only, like at school.

## [Chorus]
You look well,
and you say the same of me,
then both of us say ten past three.
You look well.

## [Verse 2]
You turn a ring I've never seen.
My last to you is one line long:
Dear Ruth, it's raining here, then nothing.
And neither of us mentions Tom.

## [Bridge]
Nineteen pulls in and it waits. Neither moves.
Rain on the roof, and a stamp in my coat.

## [Chorus]
You look well,
and you say the same of me,
then I wave on the ten past three.
You look well.
