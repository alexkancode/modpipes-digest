## Chorus (final): bars 29-36, D major
- Same melody and chords as bars 9-16. One line changes: "then I wave on the ten past three". The nineteen from the bridge is the ten past three; the narrator waves it on and stays.
- Ends on D5 held a whole bar over D on "well".

## Emotional move
From watching (verse 1: "you read the timetable, I read you") to staying (final chorus: "then I wave on the ten past three"). The words "you look well" stay the same; after verse 2 they are what both friends say instead of the reason.
