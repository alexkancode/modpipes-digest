- Lyrical, the timetable: Verse 1 line 2, bar 3, "you read the timetable, I read you". Returns as a time read off it in Chorus 1 line 3, bars 13-14, "then both of us say ten past three", and changes in the final chorus line 3, bars 33-34, "then I wave on the ten past three" (the narrator waves on the nineteen, the bus named in Bridge line 1, bars 25-26, "Nineteen pulls in and it waits. Neither moves.").
- Lyrical, rain on the shelter roof: Verse 1 line 1, bars 1-2, "Rain on the shelter roof like pennies". Returns in Verse 2 line 3, bars 21-22, as the one line of the narrator's last letter to her, "My last to you is one line long: / Dear Ruth, it's raining here, then nothing". Returns in Bridge line 2, bars 27-28, "Rain on the roof, and a stamp in my coat", where the rain sits beside the unused stamp.
- Musical, a rising fourth on the title: "look" A4 to "well" D5 (5 semitones), every time the title is sung: bars 9-10, 15-16, 29-30, 35-36. The chord moves G to D (IV to I) under the leap, so the A4 sounds as the ninth of G and the D5 lands on the tonic. No other phrase in the song rises by a fourth.
