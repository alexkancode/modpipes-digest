Step 1 of 7

Product read

Any Drive folder, instantly a slideshow.

Instideck plays the photos in a Google Drive folder as a fullscreen slideshow that advances on its own. Photos stream from Google to the browser, not through Instideck servers. Free gives 2 decks, Pro is $5/mo.

Source: Instideck landing page instideck.com · product site, third-party

Who
People with photos in Drive: families, event hosts, hobby photographers, teachers, small shops with a lobby screen. Needs a Google account.
Goal
Free signup with Google first. A Pro purchase is the deeper goal.
Live
instideck.com is live and takes payments, so there is no deploy step.

Step 2 of 7

Audience and conversations

Decision: launch groups A, B and C. Keep D and E paused for a second wave. The privacy hints (group F) join group A.

Ads show only to logged-in ChatGPT Free and Go users. People on paid seats, often professionals and school accounts, never see them. That is why D and E wait.

Source: ChatGPT ads expands across Europe openai.com · official

Each ad group holds a bid and free-form context hints that describe a moment in a conversation. Hints steer placement but guarantee nothing. In one study about 1 in 7 ads had no topical link to the prompt.

Sources: Ads developer docs developers.openai.com · official · Seer on ad relevance seerinteractive.com · third-party

Step 4 of 7

Budget and bidding

Decision: test small. $25 a day for 14 days.

Daily
$25, the platform minimum
Length
14 days from ad approval
Cap
$350 total. Set the end date, do not raise it.
Bid
Clicks (CPC), max $0.50
Where
United States, desktop_web only

Sources: Daily budgets help.openai.com · official · Placements and bidding developers.openai.com · official · Two-week test at the minimum adlibrary.com · third-party

Why clicks. You pay only for visits that can become signups. Impression bidding at a reported $25 to $65+ CPM, with CTR near 0.8 to 1.1%, can burn the budget on views.

Source: EMARKETER on CPM and CTR emarketer.com · third-party

Not conversion bidding yet. It needs a conversion event that already arrives. Switch after registration_completed has fired reliably for a week.

Sources: Conversion-optimized campaigns help.openai.com · official

Bid rules
  • Start at $0.50. The daily budget must be at least the highest bid, and $25 covers it. Daily budgets help.openai.com · official
  • No impressions after 3 days in a group: raise its bid to $1.00. Never above $1.50 in this test.
  • Decline the suggested higher bid. One tester accepted it and spent two weeks of budget in about 2 days. adlibrary test adlibrary.com · third-party
  • A, B and C share the budget. By day 7, cut a group that spent over half with the lowest signup rate.

Expected at the $0.50 start bid: about 700 clicks and 3 to 4 signups, roughly $100 each, if 0.5% of clicks sign up. The rate sources are in the table.

Step 5 of 7

Setup at ads.openai.com

Do these in order. Make the code changes in the Instideck repo and deploy them before you create the campaign.

While verification runs

  1. Sign up for self-serve Ads Manager at ads.openai.com with business details that match instideck.com and Stripe. Upload a square favicon of 256 × 256 or more (public/brand/logo-512.png). Verification takes 5 to 15 business days and billing is postpay, so do the code steps while you wait.

    Ways in help.openai.com · official · Favicon size developers.openai.com · official · Verification time webfx.com · third-party

  2. Create a Pixel in Ads Manager. Paste the exact snippet it gives into the <head> of index.html, then check in the network tab that the script loads. Allow these hosts in the CSP:

    script-src bzrcdn.openai.com; connect-src bzr.openai.com bzrcdn.openai.com; img-src bzr.openai.com

    Fire registration_completed when Google sign-in first succeeds. A consent gate exists: oaiq("consent", false). First update /privacy and the footer so the “no other server data” promise stays true.

    Measurement pixel developers.openai.com · official · Supported events developers.openai.com · official

  3. Set it at campaign level. Step 6 has the exact string to copy.

    query_string_template developers.openai.com · official

Step 6 of 7

Tracking

Count every paid visit by the /chatgpt path plus utm_source=chatgpt, never by referrer.

  1. utm_source=chatgpt&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign={campaign_id}&utm_content={ad_id}&oppref={oppref}

    Supported macros developers.openai.com · official

  2. The template has no ad-group macro, so the group rides in the target URL. These replace the root URLs in creatives.json, so every paid visit lands on its own path.

    https://instideck.com/chatgpt?utm_term=A
https://instideck.com/chatgpt?utm_term=B
https://instideck.com/chatgpt?utm_term=C

    /chatgpt already serves the landing page, so it shows up as its own path in the Cloudflare logs.

    Landing path instideck.com · product site, third-party

  3. On page load, store the UTM and oppref values in sessionStorage. Read them back when registration_completed fires.

  4. registration_completed for signup, subscription_created for Pro. The Pixel keeps oppref in a first-party __oppref cookie for 30 days.

    Supported events developers.openai.com · official · Measurement pixel developers.openai.com · official

  5. Send subscription_created from the Worker’s checkout.session.completed handler. The API does not capture oppref by itself, so pass it into Stripe Checkout metadata and read it back in the webhook.

    Conversions API developers.openai.com · official

  6. Create CHATGPT (for example, first month $2) and mention it on /chatgpt only. It counts paid conversions.

Why not referrer: advertisers have seen clicks in OpenAI’s reports with none in analytics, and one pixel showed 0 conversions while UTM analytics showed 14. Ad-review crawlers also hit tagged URLs.

Sources: Out of the Box Advisors outoftheboxadvisors.com · third-party · PerfectTablePlan successfulsoftware.net · third-party · Measurement partners help.openai.com · official

Kill criteria and review date

Decision: know when to stop. Day 0 is the day the ads are approved. Enter it to get your check dates.

  1. Do it the day approval arrives.

Stop at once if

  • spend reaches $350;
  • there are 0 signups after 150 /chatgpt sessions;
  • or after 100 sessions, time on page is under 10 s with bounce above 65%. One comparable saw 7 s and 69%.

Source: PerfectTablePlan results successfulsoftware.net · third-party

Day 3 and day 7 checks

Day 3, pause, don’t kill. If /chatgpt sessions and OpenAI’s click count differ by more than 2×, or the Pixel shows nothing while sessions exist, pause and fix tracking. Paused days don’t count.

Day 7, change creative. CTR under 0.5% after 5,000 impressions: replace the weaker ad. Over half the budget spent with signups under 0.3%: pause that group.
