Finding 1: image-fetch lost 7 of 13 downloads to rate limits

The kept gallery has 6 images. The fetch attempted 13 candidates before reaching the cap of 6. Each box below is one attempt, in list order.

123454294294294294294294296

Green: kept. Red: dropped with HTTP 429. Source: 40e0f87a/stages/image-fetch/outputs/images/images.json, field dropped. The stage stdout says kept 6, dropped 7.

  • All 7 drops are 960 px seek frames on upload.wikimedia.org. The sixth kept file is a commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath PNG on another host.
  • The drops include the only music-trait frame, Swing You Sinners at seek 180.
  • The drops also include the Betty Boop frames from Is My Palm Read and Betty in Blunderland. Those were the gallery's other chances at a Betty Boop image.
  • The v1 fetch loop calls urllib.request.urlopen back to back. The loop has no pause and no retry.
  • image-fetch began at 17:30:35.317Z. The scout's last probes and montage downloads ran until about 17:30:20Z. The rate window was still hot.

Why the tester missed the drops. The tester checked the kept images against index.md with a contact sheet. The tester never opened images.json or the fetch stdout. A search of the 3,700-line tester transcript finds "429" only in the pipeline prompt and in image bytes. Tester lesson 45 records "fetch 1 s for 6 images" as a timing. In fact 1 s for 13 attempts means fast failures.

Fix, not yet made. Sleep 2 s before each download. On a 429, wait for Retry-After and retry up to three times. Exit 1 when more than half the attempts drop. Todo 15 carries the full fix.

Finding 2: the v1 scout looked, captioned correctly, and still misfiled

The test report blames guessed seek frames and calls for opening every frame with Read. The scout transcript shows the v1 scout already looked.

  • The scout read a montage with Read three times: at 17:28:22, 17:28:53 and 17:30:22.
  • The scout titled the frame "Dizzy Dishes title card" in its own build.py. The scout did the same for a second card, "Is My Palm Read title card".
  • After the third montage the scout rewrote the seek 150 caption to "Bimbo leans at a counter beside a heavy customer". The scout kept the frame under Natwick's line.

The scout described each frame accurately and filed it under the wrong trait. The v2 sentence that matters is "a title card, a credit frame or a blank page shows no trait, so leave such frames out". The sentence "List a candidate under a trait only when the image itself shows that trait" also helps. The instruction to open every frame with Read adds cost and little else. A 320 px montage was enough for the scout to name the title card.

One v2 sentence is wrong. v2 says "The image-fetch stage keeps the first candidate per trait". The fetch code keeps the first max-images successful downloads in list order, with no trait logic. Only the scout's round-robin ordering makes the sentence roughly true.

Finding 3: the missing seventh trait is a cap and a 429, not image_target

The report says the music trait got no image because triage set image_target 6 against 7 traits. The code shows otherwise.

  • image-fetch sets cap = MODPIPES_VAR_MAX_IMAGES. No line in the fetch reads image_target.
  • image_target only steers the scout's candidate count: "aim for one and a half times image_target".
  • The scout ordered one candidate per trait before any second. With max-images 6 and 7 traits, trait 7 sits in slot 7. The cap would have cut trait 7 even with no 429.
  • In this run a 429 also dropped the music frame, so both causes acted.
  • The triage prompt asks for image_target "between 8 and max-images". With max-images 6 the request is impossible, and triage wrote 6.

The fix the report proposes, image_target at least the trait count, would change nothing. Two fixes would work: triage keeps the trait count at or below max-images, or the user sets max-images to at least the trait count. The todo's next step, max-images 8, avoids the cap by luck.
