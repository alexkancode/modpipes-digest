Corrections to the test report

  1. The criteria-array fixture was in both runs. The report says the stress toolkit invented it. The happy fixtures/sample-scores.json holds the same criteria array. The happy page kept its scorecard only because criteriaOf() at build-page.mjs line 172 accepts arrays and objects. The stress scorecardArea() at line 117 accepts arrays only.
  2. The happy demo would also have failed without its own rewrite. The happy song sets %%MIDI program 25, a steel-string guitar. The report says the happy toolkit "happened to rewrite programs to 0". That is true, and the rewrite was the only thing standing between it and the same 404.
  3. The toolkit's cost is output, not probes. See the next section. The report's "fix trims the hang only" is right. The report does not say how small that share is.

Each correction strengthens the case for freezing the toolkit. A passing run is not proof that its scripts meet the contract.

The toolkit is the critical path. Research takes 1 to 2 minutes, and the writers cannot start until the checker exists. About 600 s per run go to retyping roughly 50 KB of scripts that already exist and already pass. Freezing the toolkit saves most of those 600 s, and removes the drift that produced both stress defects.
