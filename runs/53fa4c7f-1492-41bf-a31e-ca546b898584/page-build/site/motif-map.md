- the clock above the ward door: Verse 1 line 1, "O relógio sobre a porta" (bars 1-2). It strikes three for you in line 2, "dá-te as três, enfermeira" (bars 3-4), which also names your job.
- salt: Verse 2 line 2, "cheira a sal de Alcochete" (bar 17, "sal" held on A4). The cardigan smells of the salt pans his mother worked. The salt also names her song, "fado do sal da mãe", in both first choruses (bar 11, "sal" held on D5).
- a borrowed cardigan: Verse 2 line 1, "Ele empresta-te a malha da mãe" (bars 15-16). He lends you his mother's knitted cardigan (malha).
- the word saudade: final Chorus line 2, "sem letra, saudade" (bars 24-25, "DA" held a dotted quarter on D5, beat 3). You do not know the words, so you sing the longing. Used once.
- the tram bell at 5am: final Chorus line 3, "toca o sino do elétrico" (bar 26), followed by the held "até às cinco" (bars 27-29). The tram is set up in Chorus 1 line 3 as Lisbon's own line, "o vinte e oito vem" (bar 12). In the mix, one tram bell rings on beat 1 of bar 27.
- a window that does not open: Verse 1 line 3, "a janela não se abre" (bars 5-6), with the fado turn on "não".
- descending chromatic bassline: Verse 1 and Verse 2, bars 1-6 and 15-20, chords Dm | Dm/C# | Dm/C | G/B | Gm/Bb | A7 (bass D-C#-C-B-Bb-A). It returns under the held note in the final chorus, bars 27-29: Dm | Dm/C# | Dm/C.
- 6/8 lilt in the verses against straight 4/4 in the chorus: M:6/8 at bars 1-8 and 15-22; M:4/4 at bars 9-14 and 23-29. The eighth note stays at 180 per minute, so only the grouping changes.
- held high note on the last chorus: bars 27-28, "até" on F5, tied for ten eighths over Dm then Dm/C#. It is the highest note in the song. The earlier choruses sing the same syllable on D5 (bars 9, 13, 23).
- call-and-response between voice and guitar: the voice rests for two beats after the title line in bars 10 and 14 (z4), and for three eighths in bar 24 (z3, before the pickup "sem"). In each gap the Portuguese guitar plays the hook cell back, A-A-D-C-A-G, in tremolo. Bar 22 (z3) is the guitar's answer to "e não sabes a letra", with the drums cut, before the last chorus. In the verses the guitar fills the eighth-note rests that open bars 1, 4, 5, 7, 15, 18, 19 and 21.
